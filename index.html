Recycled cotton fiber

We export recycled cotton fiber made from cotton textile waste. Reusing cotton keeps fabric out of landfill and gives manufacturers a lower-waste raw material.

If you buy fiber for yarn, nonwovens or filling, send us your requirements and we will come back with what we can supply.

Typical uses

  • Yarn spinning
  • Nonwoven fabric
  • Filling and padding
  • Wipes and cleaning cloths

What to include in your inquiry

  • Colour or shade you need
  • Quality or grade
  • Quantity, and how often
  • Destination country or port
